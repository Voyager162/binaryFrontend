### Past Projects

- ### 1. Binary Converter — Interactive Learning Tool
- To make binary numbers feel less intimidating and more intuitive, I developed a binary-to-decimal converter that brings abstract computer science concepts to life. With a clean interface and real-time bit toggling, users can visually explore how binary translates into decimal. This tool became a favorite among classmates preparing for exams, and it reinforced my passion for building tech that makes learning simpler, clearer, and more interactive.

<img src="{{site.baseurl}}/navigation/images/Converter.png" width="750px">



- ### 2. Binary Game — Bitwise Challenge
- Learning should be fun — and that’s exactly what inspired my Binary Game. Designed as a fast-paced challenge, players must match binary strings to target decimal numbers under time pressure. It transforms a core CS concept into a competitive, gamified experience. This project allowed me to combine logic, timing mechanics, and front-end development, all while making something both educational and addictive to play.

<img src="{{site.baseurl}}/navigation/images/Binary.png" width="750px">


- ### 3. LEGOLAND Media Project — Marketing and Design Pitch
- In this collaborative project, I led the media and branding strategy for a custom LEGOLAND attraction proposal. From sleek visuals to storytelling through design, I helped craft a compelling, professional pitch that impressed both peers and mentors. This experience taught me how impactful thoughtful presentation can be — and how important it is to blend creativity, clear messaging, and visual design in real-world settings.
